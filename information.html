Mission Statement

Triskelis Art is a production company based in Ignacio, Colorado. Founded in 2026, by artist and entrepreneur Jake Iberg, Triskelis’s mission is to provide a wide array of fine art products for both distinguished and casual collectors. ‘Triskelis’ is a variation on ‘triskelion,’ a Greek word defining a 3-legged or 3-pointed symbol, often depicted as a spiral, that is found in multiple cultures throughout known history.

The core philosophy of Triskelis, is that there is limitless discovery yet to be made within art, and the notion that humanity has ‘peaked’ with art, is a false one. We aim to provide a rich narrative for all audiences, while remaining vulnerable and experimental, in our artistic development. We believe in the human capacity to constantly grow, no matter one’s age or station in life, and strive to have our artistic practices reflect this, as we are constantly trying to find ways to tell stories anew.

With deep respect for the classical and traditional lineages that connect us all through time, Triskelis seeks to expand on established formulas and concepts in the art world. By incorporating new human-centered technologies, and attempting to reflect the experience of living in the Digital Age, Triskelis strives for an aesthetic of classical eloquence, infused with contemporary energy.

Artist Bio

Jake Iberg is a longtime resident of Southwestern Colorado, drawing inspiration from the region’s natural beauty. He spent much of his youth exploring the wilderness, often feeling more at home outdoors than in the classroom. From an early age, he demonstrated a strong aptitude for drawing, quickly becoming known as “the artist kid,” an identity he carries with him to this day. Although a formal pursuit of art came later, his commitment to visual expression never wavered.

He describes art as “a language above words.” After years of working in the trades, he now brings that discipline and craftsmanship into his artistic practice. Returning to college with purpose, he earned recognition for both his artwork and academic performance, reflecting a lifelong dedication to learning shaped by his upbringing.

His recent work centers on oil painting, with a focus on mood and psychological depth in still lifes and portraits. While his path has led him toward painting, he continues to explore printmaking and multimedia approaches. Increasingly, however, he finds himself drawn to the enduring tradition and discipline of classical oil painting.
